# Limitations and responsible interpretation

This project is designed as a marketing-intelligence portfolio study, not a census of all advertising in India.

1. **Source-selection bias:** campaigns that receive industry-media coverage are more likely to appear.
2. **Reporting-date bias:** `reported_date` can differ from the exact campaign launch date.
3. **Manual coding:** objective, funnel stage, sector, channel and creative strategy involve analyst judgement.
4. **Small sample:** 91 observations are useful for descriptive exploration but not for strong population-level causal claims.
5. **No outcome data:** spend, reach, sales lift, impressions, engagement and ROI are not available consistently and have not been fabricated.
6. **No causal claims:** the repository does not claim that celebrity use, AI/data usage or occasion marketing caused performance changes.
7. **Third-party ownership:** source articles and campaign assets remain owned by their publishers/brands.

## Language to use

Prefer:

> "In this 91-observation public-source sample..."

Avoid:

> "Indian brands always..." or "This proves that..."

The distinction is important for analytical credibility.
